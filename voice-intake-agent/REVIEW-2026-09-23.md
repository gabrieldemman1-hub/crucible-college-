### 1. Maya tells every caller the call is 'not recorded', but Retell stores full audio of every call, and Retell has no setting that keeps the transcript without the audio

**Severity:** Critical · **Kind:** compliance · **Verifiers agreeing:** 3 of 3

**What goes wrong.** A caller asks 'Are you recording this?' and is told no. Meanwhile Retell keeps a mixed recording, a per-speaker recording and a packet capture of the whole call, including the private handoff to staff. Penal Code 632/632.7 addresses exactly this for a California law firm. Smith v. LoanMe (2021) applies 632.7 to parties to the call, and 637.2 allows statutory damages per violation, so counsel should assess. Someone who stays on the line after hearing 'not recorded' cannot be treated as consenting to a recording. Changing the setting later does not delete audio already stored.

**Evidence.** Live agent data_storage_setting 'everything' (poc/agent/agent.config.json:26, sent by create-agent.mjs:122). All 31 calls have a recording_url. call_76de3d7b1caaba4b28c6b58f07f (v35): at 26.9 s Maya says 'Just so you know, we keep a transcript of the call, but it's not recorded.' The get-call object for that call has recording_url, recording_multi_channel_url and pcap_url (a packet capture that contains the audio). In list.json, 16 calls have a pcap_url. The SDK (agent.d.ts:252-263) offers only three values: everything; everything_except_pii (recordings kept, only PII scrubbed); and basic_attributes_only (no transcripts, recordings or logs). None of them keeps the transcript and drops the audio. The false statement appears at prompt.md:47 and :49 (Spanish), :114 ('we keep a transcript, not a recording') and :141 (confidentiality answer). It is also in build-page.mjs:216 (the footer 'Transcribed, not recorded', which is on the published Maya Call Review artifact) and DEMO.md:42 ('Is the call recorded? No'). prompts/disclosure.md:11 says the wording is only accurate if audio is discarded. The counsel sign-off table (disclosure.md:72-74) is empty, and docs/retell-setup.md item 11 is unanswered. The private handoff to staff is also recorded.

**Recommended fix.** Before any other caller reaches the number, do one of two things. (a) Replace every 'not recorded' line (prompt.md:47, 49, 114, 141; build-page.mjs:216; DEMO.md:42) with the alternate wording counsel already has in disclosure.md:43-46 ('This call may be recorded and transcribed'). (b) Build the real transcript-only design: set an agent webhook_url that, on call_ended or call_analyzed, saves the transcript to the firm's own storage, then deletes the call (DELETE /v2/delete-call/{id}) or tightens it to basic_attributes_only. PLAN-v2:283 and section 11 already plan this, and pull-calls.mjs would then read from that storage. Answer retell-setup.md item 11 first: does the webhook still carry the transcript under basic_attributes_only? If neither option can happen today, unbind the number until counsel signs off (the unbind command currently fails; see that finding). Ask counsel whether to delete the audio of the 31 existing calls (call.delete) and whether staff need notice that the handoff is recorded. After the change, make one new call and confirm that recording_url, recording_multi_channel_url and pcap_url all come back empty.
