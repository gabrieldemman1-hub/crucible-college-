## Title

Independent technical reviewer: verify an AI phone agent build (Retell AI, Node, Salesforce) for a law firm

## Description

We are a California employment law firm building an AI voice agent that answers every inbound call, collects the caller's details, logs a Salesforce lead, and warm-transfers to an intake manager. One engineer will build it from an existing plan and proof of concept. We want a second, independent expert who works alongside that engineer, reviews the work, and verifies each phase with their own tests, so that we never have to take one person's word for it.

This is a review and verification role, not a build role. You will pair with the engineer and help unblock them, but you will not write the production code, because then nobody is checking it.

**What you will do**

- Week 1: review our technical plan and proof of concept and write down where you disagree.
- Help us write the engineer job post, review the paid test-task submissions, and sit in on the interviews.
- Review every pull request before it merges. Approval means you read it, understood it, and checked the claim in the description.
- At each of four phase gates, verify independently: place your own test calls in English and Spanish, open the Salesforce records, revoke credentials mid-test to confirm the retry path, replay a webhook to confirm no duplicates, confirm in the Retell account that no audio is stored, scan the repository history for secrets. Then write a one-page signed report: what you verified, how, and what you did not.
- After cutover, operate the system for one day from the runbook without the engineer, so we know the handover is real.
- A short written note each week on what you tested and found.

**Must have**

- Shipped a voice AI agent to production yourself, on Retell AI or a comparable platform, and can show it.
- Able to place test calls to a US number and receive transfers: Retell's dashboard web call covers most of it, and a VoIP app with a US number covers the rest. Say how you would do this.
- Comfortable reading Node or TypeScript services, webhooks, and Salesforce API integrations well enough to catch a fake signature check or a silent retry bug.
- Experience reviewing other people's work and saying no with evidence. We will ask for an example.
- Clear written English for a non-technical reader.

**Independence**

You cannot be affiliated with the engineer we hire, and you cannot be the person who proposed this project to us originally. Separate contract, invoiced to us. If you also want to bid on the build, choose one role.

**Time and pay**

Roughly 5 to 7 days spread over six weeks, front-loaded at the design review and each gate, then about two hours a week for a month after launch. $35 an hour with a weekly cap of 10 hours, plus a fixed $175 for each of the four signed gate reports. About $2,100 to $2,700 in total over the build, then roughly $280 for the month after launch. Weekly call at 8 to 9 a.m. Pacific (8 to 9 p.m. in Pakistan). Start [DATE].

**To apply**, answer the screening questions.

## Screening questions

1. Describe a voice agent you shipped. What did the warm-transfer or handoff path look like, and what surprised you about the platform?
2. Describe a time you rejected a contractor's or colleague's work. What was the evidence, and what happened next?
3. A pull request says "tested end to end, all scenarios pass". What do you do before approving it?
4. Confirm you have no business relationship with any engineer you might recommend for the build role.
