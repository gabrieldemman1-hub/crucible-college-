## Title

Independent reviewer for an AI phone agent build (Retell AI, Node, Salesforce) at a law firm

## Description

We are a California employment law firm building an AI phone receptionist. It answers every inbound call, takes the caller's name and phone number, transfers them to the right staff member once that person agrees to take the call, and logs the call in Salesforce. A prototype already works on Retell AI.

One engineer will build the production system. We are not technical enough to check that work ourselves, so we want a second, independent expert who reviews everything and verifies each phase with their own tests. You are our eyes on the project.

This is a review role, not a build role. You can pair with the engineer to unblock them, but you do not write the production code, because then nobody is checking it.

**What you will do**

- Week one: review our plan and the prototype, and write down where you disagree.
- Help choose the engineer: review the paid test-task submissions and join the interview.
- Review every pull request before it merges. Approval means you read it, understood it, and checked the claim in the description.
- At each of four phase gates, verify independently: place your own test calls in English and Spanish, open the Salesforce records, revoke a credential mid-test to confirm the retry path, replay a webhook to confirm no duplicates, check the audio storage setting, scan the repository history for secrets. Then write a one-page signed report: what you verified, how, and what you did not.
- Watch the money. Each week, compare the vendor billing pages with the spend the engineer reports, and tell us at once if anything is off.
- After cutover, run the system for one day from the runbook without the engineer, so we know the handover is real.
- Send a short weekly note on what you tested and found.

**Must have**

- Shipped a voice AI agent to production yourself, on Retell AI or a comparable platform, and can show it.
- Able to place test calls to a US number and receive transfers. Tell us how you would do that.
- Reads Node or TypeScript, webhooks, and Salesforce integrations well enough to catch a fake signature check or a silent retry bug.
- Has rejected someone's work with evidence before. We will ask for an example.
- Clear written English for a non-technical reader.

**Independence.** You cannot be affiliated with the engineer we hire. Separate contract. If you would rather bid on the build, choose one role.

**Time and pay.** Roughly 5 to 7 days spread over six weeks, heaviest at the start and at each gate, then about two hours a week for a month after launch. $35 an hour with a weekly cap of 10 hours, plus a fixed $175 for each of the four signed gate reports. One 30-minute call a week at 8 a.m. Pacific. Start as soon as hired.

**To apply**, answer the screening questions.

## Screening questions

1. Describe a voice agent you shipped. How did the transfer to a human work, and what surprised you about the platform?
2. Describe a time you rejected a contractor's or colleague's work. What was the evidence, and what happened next?
3. A pull request says "tested end to end, all scenarios pass". What do you do before approving it?
4. How would you notice, within a day, that a build was running up unexpected vendor charges?
5. Confirm you have no business relationship with any engineer you might recommend for the build role.
