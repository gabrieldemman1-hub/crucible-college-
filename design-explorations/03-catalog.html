From the Registrar —

Setup, settings, and movement: taught in order, applied in real matches.

This is a structured Destiny 2 Crucible course for the Hunter class, for beginner-to-intermediate players. It assumes no competitive experience — only that you can finish a match and would like, someday, to know why it went the way it did. Configuration comes before mechanics; mechanics come before heroics.

Enrollment is open, self-paced, and free of judgment. Bring a Hunter, basic control comfort from PvE, and a tolerance for losing matches on purpose while new habits set.
