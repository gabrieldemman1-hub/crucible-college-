Who this is for

Beginner-to-intermediate Hunter mains. If you can finish a Crucible match but can't say why you lost it — or you win on instinct and want a map beneath it — this atlas was drawn for you.

Provisions required

  • Destiny 2, and one Hunter you'll main
  • Basic comfort with the controls from PvE
  • A tolerance for losing matches while new habits set
